Provenance

  • time stamps: ISO 8601 with the UTC offset (+01:00 / +02:00) read exactly and converted to UTC
  • turbine names (R80711, ...) replaced by T01 to T04 in the order of the names; no unit conversion: kW, m/s, deg, degC
  • The source does not say which instant of the interval a stamp marks; the series begins at 2014-01-01 00:00 UTC, consistent with interval starts.
  • Ba_avg is taken as the mean blade pitch angle.
  • At both autumn daylight saving changes (2014-10-26, 2015-10-25) one hour is missing in UTC, 00:00 to 01:00 UTC, the first occurrence of 02:00 to 03:00 local time: the source holds only one of the two repeated hours.

Findings describe the data, not the sensors: a long run of zeros is normal at night, a power far below the curve is normal during a stop. Thresholds are in QCConfig.
