Provenance

  • p_ac, p_dc: all values read as hectowatts (x0.1 to kW); a change to watts in the record is NOT corrected; 99th percentile of the AC power of the inverters after conversion: 58.66 of the array's kWp
  • t_amb, t_module: degrees Fahrenheit to degrees Celsius
  • wind_speed: mph to m/s
  • power_factor: raw value / 1000
  • time stamps: local standard time (UTC-8) to UTC, rounded to 15 min, moved from the end to the start of the interval
  • 4354 rows that fall on the same 15-minute stamp after rounding dropped, the first kept
  • The AC power channel is stamped about 9 minutes later than the irradiance channel (PVDAQ_SITE_NOTES.md, pv-outage-classifier).
  • Ambient temperature, wind speed and wind direction sensors are stuck for long stretches (see the quality report).
  • Tilt and azimuth are those of the array where known; the irradiance sensor of system 34 has a tilt of 5 degrees.

Findings describe the data, not the sensors: a long run of zeros is normal at night, a power far below the curve is normal during a stop. Thresholds are in QCConfig.
