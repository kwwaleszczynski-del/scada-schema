Provenance

  • sensor_x_avg, _min, _max, _std to <signal>, <signal>__min, __max, __std (mapped sensors only)
  • power and reactive power are NOT converted to kW or kvar: the benchmark scales them (anonymisation) and publishes no rated power, so they are stored as p_ac_rel and q_ac_rel, dimensionless
  • status_type_id to status_code; iec_category from the benchmark's status ids (0 normal, 1 derated, 2 idling, 3 service, 4 downtime, 5 other), an interpretation the benchmark does not publish as IEC categories
  • Active and reactive power are in the benchmark's scaled unit (p_ac_rel, q_ac_rel), not in kW: the feature description says kW, the Zenodo record says the power-related data are scaled for anonymisation and the rated power is unknown.
  • Time stamps are anonymised and shifted per dataset: they carry no calendar information.
  • farm C, event 55: one turbine's data with a training and a prediction section (x_train_test).

Findings describe the data, not the sensors: a long run of zeros is normal at night, a power far below the curve is normal during a stop. Thresholds are in QCConfig.
